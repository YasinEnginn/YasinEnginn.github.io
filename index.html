Research interests

My current graduation-project direction explores contact-aware, disruption-tolerant distributed AI inference over dynamic low Earth orbit satellite networks. The proposed work combines split image/Transformer inference with NetSatBench and ION-DTN/BPv7.

I am also interested in network programmability, network emulation, and distributed systems.
